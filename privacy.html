Privacy by architecture.

Myelin courses require no account, identity, email, analytics cookie, form submission, or server-side learner record.

What stays here

Each course stores only lesson flags, quiz attempts, completion time, score, and a random record ID in this browser’s local storage. Myelin does not receive this data.

Your controls

Each course can delete its own progress and export a JSON record to your device. AI-7 also supports local import. No course sends learner data to a Myelin server.

Do not enter

Courses contain no learner free-text fields. Do not enter or upload client, patient, student, privileged, health, proprietary, or confidential information. External links follow the destination site’s privacy practices.

Effective 16 August 2026. A future account, analytics, or server feature would require a revised notice before collection begins.
