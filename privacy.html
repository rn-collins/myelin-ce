Privacy by architecture.

Myelin requires no account, identity, email, analytics cookie, form submission, or server-side learner record.

Course data

Courses store lesson flags, written scenario exercises, quiz attempts, completion time, score, and a random record ID in this browser’s local storage. AI-7 uses myelin-ai7-v2; advanced courses use course-specific version-2 keys.

Dashboard and tools

The dashboard reads those local course keys to show progress. Field-tool entries use myelin-field-tools-v1. No course, dashboard, brief, or tool sends learner content to Myelin.

Your controls

Course records, the full learning portfolio, and field-tool records can be exported as JSON to your device. Course, tool, and portfolio deletion controls remove the corresponding local data.

Do not enter

Scenario notes and tool fields accept text only to create local exercises. Do not enter client, patient, student, privileged, health, proprietary, or confidential information. Use synthetic or appropriately minimized descriptions.
