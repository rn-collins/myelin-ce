Course data contract · version 2.1

Privacy by architecture.

AI-7 requires no account, identity, email address, analytics cookie, form submission, or server-side learner record.

What stays in your browser

The course stores only five lesson-completion flags, quiz attempt scores and timestamps, completion time, mastery score, and a randomly generated record ID in your browser's local storage under myelin-ai7-v2.

Myelin does not receive this data. It remains on this device and in this browser profile until you delete it or clear site data.

What the course does not collect

There are no fields for a learner name, email address, license number, client information, prompts, or free text. Identity is not verified. Do not enter or upload client, patient, student, legal, health, privileged, proprietary, or confidential information.

Export, import, and deletion

Export creates a JSON file on your device. Import reads a Myelin progress file locally; the file is not uploaded. The “Delete local data” control removes AI-7 progress and attempts from this browser. You can also clear site data through your browser settings.

External evidence links

Course links to official NIST, HHS, and ACCME resources open those organizations' websites. Visiting an external site is governed by that site's privacy practices and is separate from Myelin's browser-local course storage.

Effective August 16, 2026. This statement describes the current static deployment; a future account or server feature would require a revised notice before collecting learner data.
