How to use this lab

Start with the decision you actually have to make.

There is no enrolment and no order to follow. Everything on this site is readable in full without an account, so the only real question is which material matches the problem in front of you.

If you publish, file, or send it

Start with AI-7, then EV-10

AI-7 establishes the loop: separate the claim from the phrasing, open the source, check that it supports the exact proposition. EV-10 takes the same loop into study design, legal authority, and proposition-level citation. Together they cover the failure that shows up most often in public — a confident sentence with nothing behind it.

If you decide what goes into the tool

Start with PR-12, then Brief 02

PR-12 is about the moment before disclosure: what data, whose authority, which product and configuration, and what happens to it afterwards. Brief 02 is the same reasoning compressed to one page you can run in five minutes.

If you are accountable for a system

Start with GV-15, then Brief 04

GV-15 is about oversight that is real rather than ceremonial — a named reviewer, evidence they can inspect, authority to reject, and a route for affected people to contest a result. Brief 04 is the checklist version.

If you need it today

Skip to the briefs

The six field briefs are built for the decision that is already on your desk. Each states when it applies, what you need, the minimum procedure, the conditions that mean stop, and the record you must end with. Read the stop conditions first.

What commonly goes wrong

Reading the material, skipping the scenario

Every lesson ends with a written scenario for a reason. Recognising a good answer is far easier than producing one, and the gap between them is exactly what appears when you have to make the call yourself under time pressure. Write your answer before you reveal the response.

What this cannot do

It cannot tell you what your duties are

Nothing here establishes what the law, your regulator, your contract, or your professional obligations require of you. It is educational decision support: it helps you ask better questions and leave a better record. Confirm the rules that bind you with someone qualified to say.
