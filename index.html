The philosophy
Myelin is the sheath that lets a signal travel farther, faster, and without loss — and the nervous system only invests in the pathways that carry signals that matter.

That is the standard behind every Myelin CE course. We don't generate noise; we insulate the signal. Each course is built from primary sources, audited word-for-word across four version cycles, and citation-verified — so what you learn is precise, current, and defensible in your practice.

Built for rigor
  • Primary-source citations, verified against original research
  • Four-cycle correction and QA on every course
  • Timed agendas, learning outcomes, and evaluation forms per course
  • Ethics courses aligned to NASW, ACA, APA, and ABA standards
  • Neuroscience-grounded, pan-disciplinary framing
