1. Prediction, not understanding

An LLM estimates likely tokens from learned patterns. Fluent output can still be false or poorly sourced. Treat confidence in prose as presentation—not evidence.

2. Retrieval is not a truth guarantee

Retrieval can supply documents at response time, but a model can misread, omit, or overstate them. Open the source and confirm the exact proposition.

3. A verification loop

  1. Remove sensitive data.
  2. Ask for a bounded draft.
  3. Check material claims against authoritative sources.
  4. Apply domain judgment and document human review.
